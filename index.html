About Our Trust & School

Kids Vatika Smart School is a premier social educational institution established and operated by Viksit Bharat Educational & Welfare Trust, a registered public charitable trust based in Baltana, Zirakpur (SAS Nagar, Punjab). Our institution was founded with the conviction that quality primary education is a fundamental human right, not a privilege reserved for the financially secure.

Operating independently in a private premises with a strict zero-donation policy, the school serves students from Playway, Nursery, and Kindergarten up to Class 5th. We support children belonging to Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), marginalized groups, and minority backgrounds, ensuring that financial limitations never stop a student from learning, growing, and succeeding.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To cultivate an inclusive and equitable educational ecosystem across India where financial deprivation does not hinder any child's cognitive or social growth. In alignment with national development and Prime Minister Narendra Modi's inspiring motto, "Padhega India, Tabhi Badhega India," we envision a knowledgeable, self-reliant, and empowered youth community.

Our Mission

To reach every underserved child in our locality and equip them with modern primary education. We eliminate financial barriers by providing free uniforms, shoes, textbooks, and stationery, while building an environment focused on academic excellence, personal dignity, and character formation.
