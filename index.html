Hamare Baare Mein (About the NGO)

Kids Vatika Smart School ek pramukh educational initiative hai jiska sanchalan Viksit Bharat Educational & Welfare Trust dwara kiya jata hai[cite: 4, 7]. Zirakpur aur aas-paas ke ilaqo me yeh trust under-privileged aur minor community ke bachho ko free aur concessional schooling pradan karti hai[cite: 4, 7].

Filhal school private premises me bina kisi donation ke operate kiya ja raha hai, jahan Nursery se Class 5th tak ke bachho ke sarvangeen vikas par dhyan diya jata hai[cite: 7].

Vision & Mission

Hamara Vision

Ek aise samaj ka nirman jahan har Indian student ko samman aur shiksha ka pura adhikar mile[cite: 7]. Zirakpur ke kisi bhi bachhe ki aarthik tangee unke padhai ke beech na aaye — "Padhega India, Tabhi Badhega India."

Hamara Mission

Vanchit aur minority parivaron ke bachho tak standard syllabus, free kitabein aur uniforms pahunchakar primary level par hi unka academic base strong banana[cite: 7].
